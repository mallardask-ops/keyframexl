# Research brief, 2026-11-15 (shared context for research agents)

KeyFrameXL is an FL Studio–like sequencer for Linux, written in Rust (eframe/egui 0.31, egui_dock 0.16, cpal).
Repo root: /home/mallar2d/.t3/projects/keyframexl

## What exists today
- Shell: top "deck" (transport, 7-seg LCD, tempo/swing knobs, Open/Save/Export), left Browser (workspace presets,
  window list, patterns, channels), docked/floating windows in the centre (egui_dock), status bar.
  Code: src/ui/mod.rs, deck.rs, browser.rs, workspace.rs.
- Windows: Playlist (src/ui/playlist.rs), Channel rack (src/ui/rack.rs), Piano roll per channel (src/ui/roll.rs),
  Instrument window with our own DirectWave-like Sampler (src/ui/instrument.rs, src/ui/sampler.rs).
- Engine: voice engine with block rendering (src/engine.rs), sampler model (src/sampler.rs), song model v1
  (src/sequence.rs; 16 steps, ticks), format 2 draft (src/core/). No mixer, no effects yet.
- Widgets kit: src/ui/kit.rs (raised/sunken chrome, TE-style knob encoders, chips, buttons, LCD).
  Theme: src/theme.rs.
- Rules: docs/ui-rules.md (UI rules: BMS design kit + late Teenage Engineering vibe + FL canon).
  Roadmap: docs/roadmap.md. Prior research: research_notes/fl-toolbar-inventory.md, research_notes/directwave-params.md.
- Native screenshots of our app: artifacts/*.png (run `ls artifacts`).

## The user's complaint
The current piano roll and playlist are close to unusable: everything snaps to rigid ticks, editing takes
too many clicks, there are no FL-style window menus. The user wants FL-level scale and layout.
