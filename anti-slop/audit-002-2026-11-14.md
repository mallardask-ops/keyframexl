# BMS application pass: audit and verification

Scope: native eframe application at 1366×768, IBM Plex Sans/Mono, Rack and Piano Roll. User authorized routine design decisions and fixes in this pass.

1. **HIGH — ambiguous transport state (R-25/C-4), fixed.** Primary transport has a BMS red fill and white label, Stop uses bright text with a visible border, Pattern/Song selection uses an explicit filled state. White on #BE2A1E measures 5.93:1.
2. **HIGH — rhythm and playback position conflated (C-4), fixed.** Enabled events are filled and underlined; playback is a moving white frame and geometric caret. Four groups have their own background and beat labels. Inactive control boundary #6A6D72 on #222326 measures 3.03:1 (non-text contrast); muted labels #A3A6AB on that surface measure 6.44:1.
3. **MEDIUM — Rack compressed into corner and bass detached (R-31/C-3), fixed.** The timeline spans the main workspace; names and all time columns share alignment. Demo Bass is a full-height fourth row with its note preview and direct Piano Roll entry. Both its label and timeline respond to click and expose keyboard focus.
4. **HIGH — pitch alignment and missing glyphs (C-5/C-4), fixed.** Piano Roll uses a chromatic scale, note and slide coordinate mapping, ASCII sharp labels and geometric slide/cursor triangles. Actual screenshots were inspected; C3 and C4 align with their key rows and the trajectory reaches the slide's right edge.
5. **MEDIUM — font source uncertain (R-06), fixed.** Embedded IBM Plex Sans Regular/SemiBold and Plex Mono files come from the supplied kit and retain their OFL notice. Native text uses the configured font families.
6. **MEDIUM — velocity hidden (C-3), fixed.** Toolbar sets velocity for new notes, and a dedicated lane edits existing events. This makes the DWP velocity split audible without editing project JSON.

Evidence: artifacts/rack-1366x768.png and artifacts/piano-roll-1366x768.png are native application framebuffer captures, not HTML reconstructions. Captures use demo data, running transport, 100% scale and a 1366×768 client area; no unrelated desktop window is captured. A 125% KDE-scale session, screen-reader behaviour and a broad keyboard-only audit remain unverified. No claim is made for those checks.
