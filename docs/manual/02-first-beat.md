# Your first beat

A beat in ten minutes, from an empty project to a WAV file.

## 1. Start

`Ctrl+Shift+N` makes a new project. It already has three channels from
the built-in KFX Core kit: Kick, Snare and Hat. Each is routed to its own
mixer insert.

## 2. Program the drums

Open the channel rack (`F6`). Each row is a channel, each square a step
(a 16th note).

- Click a step to switch it on; click again to switch it off.
- `Shift+click` a step cycles its velocity.
- Right-click a step clears it.

Put the kick on steps 1, 5, 9 and 13, the snare on 5 and 13, the hat on
every odd step. Press `Space` to play. `Space` again stops and rewinds;
`Ctrl+Space` pauses where it is.

## 3. Add a bass

*Add → Channel → KeyFrame Tide* adds a synthesiser. Pick a preset from its
header (the bass category), then open the piano roll (`F7`) and draw notes
with the draw tool (`P`). Drag a note's right edge to change its length.

## 4. Make a song

`F4` jumps to the next empty pattern; `F2` renames it. Put a variation of
the drums there. Open the playlist (`F5`), pick a pattern and click in a
track to place a clip. `L` switches between playing the current pattern
and playing the song.

## 5. Mix

Open the mixer (`F9`). Each insert has a fader, pan, mute and up to ten
effect slots. Click an empty slot to add an effect; a reverb on the snare
or a compressor on the master is a good start.

## 6. Export

`Ctrl+S` saves the project as a `.kfxl` file. `Ctrl+R` exports audio:
WAV, FLAC or MP3, of the song or of the current pattern.
