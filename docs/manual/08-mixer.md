# Mixer and effects

Open the mixer with `F9`. Each channel plays into an insert (set in the
channel rack); inserts play into the master.

## Inserts

- Fader, pan, and the mute lamp. Right-click or `Ctrl+click` the lamp
  solos the insert; again un-solos.
- Ten effect slots. Click an empty slot to add an effect; click a slot's
  lamp to bypass it. Each slot has a mix knob, and the mix can be
  automated.
- **Sends**: *Add send to* sends the insert to another insert (a shared
  reverb, a bus) with its own level.
- **Sidechain input**: an effect such as the compressor can listen to
  another insert. A slot that does shows `SC` and the key insert's number;
  the insert lists what ducks it.
- Track and effect presets: *Save track preset…*, *Load track preset…*,
  and the same for single effects. *Reset selected track* clears one.

## The 32 effects

- **EQ and filters**: Parametric EQ 2, 3-band EQ, Tilt EQ, Filter.
- **Dynamics**: Compressor, Multiband Compressor, Limiter, Squeeze, Soft
  Clipper, Noise Gate, Transient Shaper.
- **Delay and reverb**: Reverb, Room Reverb, Convolution Reverb, Bloom,
  Delay, Stereo Delay.
- **Distortion and colour**: Drive, Wavefolder, Bitcrusher.
- **Modulation and time**: Chorus, Hyper Chorus, Phaser, Tremolo, Auto
  Pan, Ring Modulator, Time.
- **Stereo and vocal**: Stereo, Stereo Shaper, Mid / Side, Balance,
  Vocoder.

Every effect has its own face and A/B compare. Plugin effects (CLAP,
VST3, VST2) go in the same slots through *External plugins*.
